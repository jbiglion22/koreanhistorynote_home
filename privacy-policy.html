한국어 Korean History Note

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Summary Korean History Note (한국사 노트) does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location. There is no sign-up or login. Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob, and in doing so Google processes device information such as the advertising ID (see section 5).

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Android app Korean History Note (한국사 노트, package name com.phonensoft.koreanhistorynote, the "App").

2. Personal information we collect

The developer does not collect or store personal information. The App has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run any server. Information that Google processes for ads is described in section 5.

3. Information stored only on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information only on your phone. It does not identify you and is never sent anywhere.

Saved cardsThe person, event and term cards you saved
Recently viewedCards you opened recently (shown again on the search screen)
Questions already seenQuestions you already opened on the home screen (so the same questions are not repeated)
Display modeYour choice of system default, light or dark

This information is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your phone's settings.

4. Permissions

The App does not ask for location, contacts, camera or storage permissions. It includes the following normal permissions for ads, which do not require your approval.

  • Internet and network state: used to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google to show ads and measure their performance.

All history content is built into the App, so you can read it without an internet connection.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob ads are shown on the App's home screen, search results, saved card list, person/event/term cards, work screens and the exit confirmation screen. To show ads, Google may process the following information.

Processed byGoogle LLC (Google AdMob)
InformationAdvertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, ad impressions and clicks, etc.
PurposeShowing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks

For how Google handles this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

  • If you do not want personalized ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Privacy > Ads.
  • In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the App asks for your consent on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy settings.
  • The developer does not receive or store any ad-related information. The App does not include any usage analytics tools.

6. Contact email

The App has no feature for sending email. The email address and content of any email you send to the address in section 9 are used only to reply to you, and are deleted without delay once the reply is complete.

7. Children's privacy

The developer does not collect personal information from any user, including children. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of General audiences (G).

8. Changes to this policy

If the App's features change and this policy needs to be updated, the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.
